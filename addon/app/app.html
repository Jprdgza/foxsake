      Before your first install
      
      
      Converted extensions aren't signed by Mozilla, so the browser has to allow unsigned add-ons:
        open about:config, search for xpinstall.signatures.required and set it to false.
        Zen, Firefox Developer Edition, Nightly and ESR support this; regular Firefox doesn't.
      
      
      Everything happens in your browser. Fox Sake only talks to Google's servers, to download the extension you picked
        and to check converted extensions for updates. Converted extensions are other people's software: keep them for your own use.
